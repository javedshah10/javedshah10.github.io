About Me

I'm Javed Shah, a Gen AI system developer and data analyst in Riyadh. I like to build things: AI agents, automations, Power BI dashboards, and this website.

For six years I've worked in Saudi logistics, turning operational and sales data into reports that managers use to make decisions. Before that I spent five years in GIS, building 3D models and terrain surfaces from LiDAR data for Jeddah's streets and the Saudi Arabia Railway.

Most of my spare hours now go into generative AI. I build small apps, automation workflows and my own AI agents and harnesses with Claude Code, Cursor and Grok, and I help businesses automate their reports and routine work.

I enjoy the creative side as much as the numbers. I directed this site myself, from the first storyboard to the last animation.
